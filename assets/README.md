# Assets

This folder is where optional media for the site lives. The site works fine without anything in here — these are purely upgrades.

- **`intro.mp4`** — a short personal intro clip (15–45s) shown in the circular player next to your bio, on click ("hit play — that's me"). It has sound, so record with audio — this one isn't muted/looping like a background video, it behaves like a real video player. Drop a file with this exact name here and it activates automatically. Until then, the circle shows a plain gold fill and the play button simply does nothing when clicked.
- **`intro-poster.jpg`** — a still frame shown before you press play. Optional, but recommended (use a frame from the video, or a headshot cropped to a square).

Tips for the video:
- Keep the file small (under ~20MB) so the page loads fast — export at 1080p, moderate bitrate.
- Square or portrait framing works best since it's cropped into a circle — keep your face centered.
